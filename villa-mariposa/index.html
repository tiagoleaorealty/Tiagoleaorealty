The Residence

A rare sanctuary of design, serenity, and location — where architecture, nature, and comfort exist in perfect harmony.

Villa Mariposa is a refined tropical residence in the heart of Guanacaste’s prestigious Gold Coast, blending Balinese-inspired architecture with the lush beauty and relaxed lifestyle of the coast. With 341 m² (3,670 sq ft) of total construction including the pool, the villa balances generous indoor comfort with true outdoor living.

Four spacious bedrooms — each with its own private en-suite bath — open to gardens, terraces, and water. At the center, a designer pool framed by sun terraces and social spaces sets the stage for entertaining, quiet mornings, and warm Pacific evenings. And the generous, tree-canopied lot leaves room to grow: space for a second home, with plans already drawn.

Ideal as a full-time residence, luxury vacation home, or high-performing investment in one of the region’s fastest-growing destinations.
