The Opportunity

Scale like this, this close to everything, is rare on the Central Pacific.

Finca Herradura is 82.5 hectares of rolling pasture and forested ridgeline with ocean views, public road frontage and internal roads already in place. Los Sueños Resort and Marina is two kilometers away, Jacó is eight minutes down the road, and the supermarket is one minute from the gate.

The land has residential and commercial land use, water availability for a project of up to 500 homes, and electrical service available. It is the scale a master-planned community, a resort or a phased mixed-use project needs, in a corridor where the buyers, the marina and the highway to San José already exist.
